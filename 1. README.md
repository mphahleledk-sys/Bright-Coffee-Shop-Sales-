☕ Bright Coffee Shop — Sales Decline Analytics

Diagnostic analysis of a 6-month revenue decline across three coffee shop locations

📖 Overview

Over a six-month period, Bright Coffee Shop experienced a consistent decline in revenue across its three store locations. Senior leadership required a data-driven explanation of the decline and actionable recommendations to improve performance.

This project applies business analytics, SQL, Excel, Power BI, and data storytelling techniques to identify the underlying causes of declining sales and provide strategic recommendations for recovery.

🎯 Project Goal

To determine the primary drivers behind declining revenue and provide practical recommendations that can improve sales performance, customer engagement, and operational efficiency.

Key Questions
Is the decline seasonal or structural?
Which products are driving or limiting revenue growth?
Which locations are performing best and worst?
Are customer purchasing behaviours changing?
What operational improvements can reverse the trend?

🚨 Root Causes of Revenue Decline

The analysis identified three primary drivers:

1. Time-of-Day Revenue Concentration

Revenue is heavily dependent on morning traffic.

Risk: Any decline in morning footfall has a disproportionate impact on overall revenue.

2. Product Mix Weaknesses

Several products underperform despite occupying inventory and menu space.

Example: Dark Chocolate products contribute significantly less revenue than coffee and tea offerings.

3. Untapped Customer Growth Opportunities

Most revenue comes from low-spending customers.

Opportunity: Increase average order value through targeted promotions and loyalty programs.

💡 Recommendations
Operations
Implement automated KPI tracking across all locations
Monitor daily revenue, transactions, and average basket size
Introduce afternoon and evening promotional campaigns
Create dinner-hour specials to increase off-peak traffic
Product Strategy
Promote underperforming products (e.g., Dark Chocolate) through bundled offers
Consider modest price increases on top-performing coffee products
Expand successful organic product lines
Launch weekend-focused combo promotions

Example:

Buy 2 Drinks, Get 1 Free

Customer Engagement
Launch a WhatsApp feedback channel
Encourage customer reviews and service ratings
Strengthen social media marketing across:
TikTok
Facebook
Instagram
X (Twitter)
Introduce loyalty rewards aimed at increasing basket size among low-spending customers

📌 Executive Summary

The decline in sales is primarily driven by:

Over-reliance on morning trading hours
Weak performance of selected product categories
Underutilized customer growth opportunities
Limited revenue generation during afternoons, evenings, and weekends

By implementing targeted promotional strategies, optimizing product offerings, and improving customer engagement, Bright Coffee Shop can reduce revenue concentration risk and create sustainable growth across all locations.
